## Design Specificity Verdict

**Authored for this product, not category-interchangeable.** The Graticule draws the runner-up as a
position and the threshold as a labelled tick, so separability reads as distance before it reads as
arithmetic. No generic dashboard kit contains that control because no generic product has the
"0.91 against 0.89" problem. `/manager/request` puts an engineering blocker on the screen's face and
enumerates Live / After / Never rather than rendering an empty panel.

**Deterministic scan:** `detect.mjs --json web/app web/components` returned `[]`, exit 0. Zero findings.
Browser sweep of 14 routes found no console errors, no horizontal overflow, no unintended clipping,
no broken images. Focus rings verified present on every queue action by real Tab presses, 6.24:1
against the card.

The gap is not the visual world. It is that several of the product's own rules exist as constants and
comments but were never wired to a control.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Bulk approve is irreversible, unconfirmed and silent
`components/console/QueueBoard.tsx`. The handler writes every eligible id into `decided` and never
calls `setLast`, so the undo banner never appears and `U` is inert. Verified live: 9 rows to 5 rows,
`U` does nothing. PRODUCT.md says per-row accountability is the only reason a planner can defend a
decision six months later.
**Fix:** confirm sheet listing eligible rows, then a `last` of kind `bulk` holding the id array so `U`
reverses the batch as one gesture.

### [P0] Reject carries no reason, and the reason list already exists
`lib/status.ts` declares `REJECT_REASONS`; nothing renders it. The console can produce a rejection the
field surface has no text for, while `/field/updates` already renders "Not accepted: duplicate".
Alignment rule 8 says every rejection carries a reason.
**Fix:** `X` opens an inline reason strip bound to `1`-`4`, plus an optional note. Nothing leaves the
queue until a reason is chosen.

### [P0] SOS is unreachable by keyboard or screen reader, and its confirmation is a dead end
`components/field/Sos.tsx` binds only pointer events. `Enter` does nothing, `.click()` does nothing.
TalkBack's double-tap synthesises a click, not a sustained press, so a supervisor using a screen
reader cannot raise an emergency at all. After `cancelLeft` hits 0 the panel covers the tab bar at
`z-50` with no dismissal and the SOS control is gone from the DOM, contradicting the component's own
"no rate limit, ever" comment.
**Fix:** key handlers driving the same hold timer, an AT confirm fallback, `role="alert"` for progress
and sent state, and a collapsing banner that leaves the tab bar reachable and SOS live.

### [P0] The field surface breaks both of its own hard floors
Measured at 375x812. The sun switch is **28x28px**, half the stated 56px minimum, and it is the one
control the direction contract names as the answer to direct sun. **34 elements below the 18px
functional floor** on `/field` alone: Done/Started/Blocked at 14.5px, all four tab labels and the SOS
label at 12.5px, schedule chips at 12.5px.
**Fix:** an 18px inherited base inside `app/field/**`, control labels at `--text-title` or above, and a
56px two-segment sun switch built for this surface rather than the console component shrunk.

### [P0] Avatar initials fail contrast at 2.58:1
Measured: `--ground` on `--ramp-1` = **2.58:1** at 11px, below even the 3:1 non-text floor;
`--ramp-2` = 4.21:1. `globals.css` annotates every ink and accent token with its ratio but leaves
`--ramp-1` through `--ramp-4` unannotated, and the ramp is being used as a text background.

### [P1] `/` and `?` send a question to a supervisor
`QueueBoard.tsx` maps both to Ask. There is a search input in the top bar, `/` focuses search in every
tool a planner already uses, and `?` is the universal help key. Asking is the only planner action
allowed to interrupt a low-motivation user, bound to the two keys most likely to be pressed by reflex.
**Fix:** Ask moves to `C`, `/` focuses search, `?` opens a shortcut overlay.

### [P1] LogFlow echoes words the user did not type
`components/field/LogFlow.tsx` hardcodes the confirm-back as "Hydro test on the condensate line" and
"Matched to Hydro test condensate line A" regardless of input. The one screen the product calls the
attestation and the audit anchor contradicts what the user just typed, with no fixture label on the
field surface.

## Persona Red Flags

**The discipline supervisor / Casey (distracted mobile)** — the persona this surface exists for.
28px sun switch, gloved thumb outdoors, and it is the control that makes the screen readable outdoors.
SOS label at 12.5px, dark red on a near-white bar in the light ground: the least visible element on
screen is the emergency control, in the condition it was designed for. "Not accepted, tap to see why"
is a plain `span` with no handler. `/field/updates` shows the question and no way to answer it.

**Sam (accessibility-dependent).** Zero `aria-live` regions in the codebase. Queue cards are not
focusable, all nine return `tabIndex -1`, so the `aria-current` cursor moves visually but focus never
follows. `StatusChip` puts each status's meaning in a native `title`, invisible to touch and keyboard.
`SignalBars` has no aria attributes at all while the Gauge beside it does this correctly.

**Riley (stress tester).** Bulk approve then `U`: four rows gone, undo dead. Hold SOS, wait eleven
seconds: permanently trapped. Send SOS then Cancel: one tap undoes what took a two-second hold.
OTP `123`: field silently empties, no message. OTP `1234`: verifies, on a field labelled "Six digit
code". Resend twice: the no-code fallback appears after zero failed attempts. Every button on
`/manager` is inert with no feedback, while the console's equivalents say "Nothing was written".

## Minor Observations

- Provenance is absent from `/console/coverage`, `/console/blockers`, `/console/activities`,
  `/manager/emergency` and the whole field surface, all of which carry figures.
- `--ink-dim` at 3.3-4.3:1 carries functional content: phone numbers, timestamps, quantities, and the
  entry surface's authentication disclosure at 12.5px.
- `/console/activities/[id]` renders `document.title` as the root fallback "TRACE"; every other route
  sets a specific title.
- The theme is one global localStorage key, so a supervisor flipping the sun switch also flips the
  planner console on the same device.
- Undo is single-level and the banner never dismisses, so after two approvals it claims an undo
  already consumed against a newer row.
- House rule held: no em or en dash in any visible copy. Six statuses held. No activity ID on `/field`.
  `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured globally.

## Questions to Consider

- What if bulk approve were a staging list rather than an action? The floor slider would become a live
  lens on the queue rather than a loaded gun, and the preview, undo and accountability problems all
  dissolve at once.
- Should the field surface carry a provenance disclosure at all? A supervisor cannot act on "this
  score is hand authored". But LogFlow's fabricated echo is a different thing entirely.
- What is a supervisor's route to disputing a rejection? "Nothing is ever discarded" is promised twice,
  and a rejection with a reason and no reply is still a one-way door.
- The sun switch is a manual control for a condition the device can measure.
